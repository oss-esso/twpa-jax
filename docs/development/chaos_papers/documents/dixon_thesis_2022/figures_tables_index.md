As previously discussed the higher frequency tone is also larger in amplitude and is referred to as the pump. The lower frequency tone is lower in amplitude and is the signal. The standard operating procedure is to hold the pump at a fixed frequency, while sweeping the signal frequency over some bandwidth we wish to measure in. Since we wish to observe potential Sum Frequency Generation (SFG) between the two input tones and their mixing products we again must consider the bandwidth of our experimental set up, shown in fig. 3.22. As such, for all subsequent measurements the pump frequency fp = 6.0132 GHz, while the signal frequency is swept from 2 < fs < 4 GHz. The same fitting routine described in section 3.1.3 is used.
