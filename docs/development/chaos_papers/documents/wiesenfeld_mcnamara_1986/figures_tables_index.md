### Figure 6 — caption on PDF page 7

Figure 6 shows the output of a spectrum analyzer when the modulation frequency Q was close to 3'/2 and illus- trates a number of qualitative features. The input power level is —50 dB, while the output power level at this fre- quency is —25 dB, yielding a gain in power by a factor of about 300. This represents a relatively modest amplitude amplification, roughly by a factor of 17. As expected, the input at the single frequency Q gives rise to a series of pairs of lines, centered at co/2, 3'/2, 5'/2, etc., but no lines near integer multiples of co. Finally, the partners within each pair of lines have nearly equal amplitude, in reasonable agreement with Eq. (3.28), which predicts ex- act equality.
