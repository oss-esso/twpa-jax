AMPLIFIER NOISE In all externally pumped parametric amplifiers based on the Josephson junction one encounters the strange phe- nomenon of the noise rise which at least for tunnel junctions seems to have the unfortunate consequence that gain and noise temperature are proportional. For a detailed review of this we should refer to Ref. 10. For the microbridge at least the range of parameters is such that it seems very unlikely that chaotic noise might occur. The RC time constant corre- sponds to a frequency of order 500 GHz. The pump frequen- cy is therefore well below the lower cut-off frequency for chaotic behavior.
