\chapter{Conventions and the Errors They Guard Against}
\label{chap:conventions}

Every convention below has a specific failure mode attached. They are collected
here because each one, violated, produces a result that looks plausible.

\section{Phasor and reconstruction}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.30\textwidth} p{0.30\textwidth} p{0.32\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Convention} & \textbf{Statement} & \textbf{Failure if violated} \\
\midrule
Positive phasor
& $\xvec(t)=2\Real\sum_k X_k e^{+ik\wp t}$
& Factor-of-two errors in every amplitude; sign errors in the conjugate coupling
  if $-i$ is assumed. \\
\addlinespace
Reconstruction factor
& Always $2$; recorded as \code{real\_reconstruction\_factor} in the saved
  solution
& An imported pump solution written with factor $1$ gives half the pump
  amplitude and a gain curve that looks like a lower-power operating point. \\
\addlinespace
Source amplitude
& $\Svec_k = \tfrac12\lambda I\,\vect{e}_p\,\delta_{k,k_{\mathrm{src}}}$
& Factor of two in drive; the pump solve converges to the wrong point without
  any error. \\
\addlinespace
Reduced flux quantum
& $\rphi = \fluxquantum/2\pi$ everywhere
& A factor of $2\pi$ inside $\sin(\psi/\rphi)$; the device appears far more or
  less nonlinear than it is. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{Circuit stamping}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.30\textwidth} p{0.30\textwidth} p{0.32\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Convention} & \textbf{Statement} & \textbf{Failure if violated} \\
\midrule
Josephson branches
& Represented only through $\Bphi$ and $\Ic$; never stamped into $\Kmat$
& Double-counted stiffness. Passive response still looks fine; gain collapses. \\
\addlinespace
Junction capacitance
& $C_J$ \emph{is} stamped, into $\Cmat$
& Wrong plasma frequency, hence wrong dispersion, hence wrong phase matching. \\
\addlinespace
SNAIL branch law
& Shifted to its solved static equilibrium; slope carried entirely by the
  branch
& If the small-signal slope is also stamped into $\Kmat$, the same
  double-counting as above. This was a live defect. \\
\addlinespace
$C_J$ from $L_J$
& $C_J^{(j)} = C_J^{\mathrm{nom}} L_J^{\mathrm{nom}}/L_J^{(j)}$, so $\omega_J$
  is constant
& Position-dependent plasma frequency; unintended dispersion along the line. \\
\addlinespace
$C_J$ scatter
& Independent, and \emph{deliberately} breaks the above relation
& ``Fixing'' it to track $L_J$ removes a real fabrication effect. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{Ports and power}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.30\textwidth} p{0.30\textwidth} p{0.32\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Convention} & \textbf{Statement} & \textbf{Failure if violated} \\
\midrule
Norton termination
& On-chip power is \emph{not} $I^{2}Z_0/2$
& Overstates on-chip pump by \SI{6.02}{\dB}. Accounted for most of an apparent
  \SI{7.9}{\dB} model--measurement gap. \\
\addlinespace
Pump port
& Pump at port 4; signal $1\to2$
& Using port 1 for both leaves a promoted-pump residual of $\sqrt{2}$. \\
\addlinespace
Depletion port
& State the port, or use the all-port net quantity
& A published set of depletion figures was read at a port carrying
  \SI{9.1}{\percent} of the pump. \\
\addlinespace
Peak current
& $I_{\mathrm{peak}}=\sqrt{2P/Z_0}$, with
  \code{--pump-current-jc-scale 1.0}
& The parser default of \code{2.0} is a historical parity convention; leaving it
  doubles the drive. \\
\addlinespace
Line loss
& Use the measured model \eqref{eq:loss-model}
& Omitting it understates attenuation by \SI{34.54}{\dB} at
  \SI{7.1}{\giga\hertz}. Two campaigns ran with none. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{Readout}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.30\textwidth} p{0.30\textwidth} p{0.32\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Convention} & \textbf{Statement} & \textbf{Failure if violated} \\
\midrule
Gain-vs-off
& Ratio of voltage-per-input-current responses
& The absolute one-port $S$ is affine (carries the $-1$); differencing two
  absolute values does not give a ratio. \\
\addlinespace
Multitone gain
& Through \code{tone\_s21}, as pump-on/pump-off
& The $|i\omega X|/V_{\mathrm{in}}$ path was biased by \SI{12.041}{\dB}. \\
\addlinespace
Quantum efficiency
& Requires the ladder basis; reweight by $\sqrt{\omega_n/\omega_m}$
& Applying \eqref{eq:qe} to the classical $S$ silently gives wrong numbers. \\
\addlinespace
Idler voltage
& Extracted at the idler's own signed frequency
& Using $\ws$ misscales the idler. \\
\addlinespace
S-matrix indexing
& \code{S[frequency, output port, source port]}
& Transposed port indices give a plausible but wrong directivity. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\midrule

& \textbf{Valid} invariant, real floor $\approx\SI{2.2}{\percent}$. \\
\addlinespace
\code{all\_tone\_manley\_rowe\_rel\_err}

\section{Terminology}

\begin{description}
\item[Convergence failure, not ``fold''.] A fold is a property of the solution
  branch; a convergence failure is a property of the solver. Use the latter
  unless the physics has been verified. Earlier work conflated them.

\item[Basis truncation, not ``converged''.] A result computed at $S=10$ is a
  result \emph{at $S=10$}, not a converged result, until self-convergence has
  been measured.

\item[Dead band, not ``no compression point''.] Where there is no gain,
  $P_{1\mathrm{dB}}$ is undefined; the status
  \code{NO\_GAIN\_AT\_OPERATING\_POINT} is reported instead of a number.
\end{description}

\section{Operational rules}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Circuits come from \file{designs/}, never from an output directory. An
  output directory is a snapshot, not a design.
\item Run the full test suite, including the slow physics gates, with a
  temporary directory outside the repository:
\begin{lstlisting}[language=bash]
python -m pytest -q -p no:cacheprovider --basetemp <tmp> --run-slow
\end{lstlisting}
  Running without \code{--run-slow} is not complete validation.
\item Verify from a clean clone, not from the working tree: files removed from
  version control remain importable locally.
\item Demand that every new gate be seen failing before it is trusted.
\item Set \code{TWPA\_REQUIRE\_PARDISO=1} during timed campaigns so a silent
  fallback becomes a hard error.
\end{enumerate}
